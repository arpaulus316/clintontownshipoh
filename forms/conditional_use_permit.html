What is a Conditional Use Permit? A conditional use permit allows for a land use that is permitted within a zoning district only when specific conditions are met. The Board of Zoning Appeals reviews each request to ensure the proposed use is appropriate for the location and will not adversely affect surrounding properties or the general welfare of the community.

Permits & Applications

Clinton Township requires permits for various types of construction, signage, and land use changes. Below you'll find information about required permits, applications, and associated fees.

Zoning Permit

A zoning permit is required before beginning construction or changing the use of a property. The permit ensures that your project complies with township zoning regulations.

  • Required for all new construction, additions, and structural alterations
  • Required for changes in property use
  • Must be obtained before starting work
  • Inspections may be required during and after construction
Download Zoning Permit Application

Variance Application

A variance allows deviation from specific zoning requirements when strict application would cause unnecessary hardship. Variance applications are reviewed by the Board of Zoning Appeals.

  • Applicant must demonstrate practical difficulty or unnecessary hardship
  • Application must be filed with the Zoning Secretary
  • Public hearing will be scheduled before the Board of Zoning Appeals
  • Adjacent property owners will be notified
Download Variance Application

Sign Permit

Sign permits ensure that signs comply with size, placement, and safety regulations. Most permanent signs require a permit before installation.

  • Required for most permanent exterior signs
  • Temporary signs may be exempt (check with Zoning Officer)
  • Submit site plan showing sign location and dimensions
  • Signs must meet setback and height requirements
Download Sign Permit Application

Zoning Permit Process - Step-by-Step Guide

About Zoning Permits

Before beginning any construction, expansion, alteration, or change of use for buildings or structures, you must obtain a zoning permit from Clinton Township.

Purpose: Zoning permits ensure that all development complies with township zoning regulations, protecting property values and maintaining community standards.

When Required: All new construction, additions, structural changes, accessory buildings, and changes in property use require a zoning permit.

1

Obtain Application Form

Get a Zoning Permit Application from the Zoning Officer or download it from the township website.

Contact Information: Jim Bowser, Zoning Officer
Phone: 638-2587
Address: 2588 Beck Road
Office Hours: Contact the Zoning Officer to schedule an appointment or discuss your project
2

Complete Application

Fill out the application form completely with all required information:

  • Property information: Address, parcel number, lot dimensions
  • Project details: Type of construction, dimensions, intended use
  • Owner information: Name, address, contact details
  • Contractor information: If applicable
3

Prepare Required Documents

Gather all necessary documentation to submit with your application:

Site Plan Showing property boundaries, existing structures, proposed construction, setbacks, and dimensions
Building Plans Architectural drawings or sketches of the proposed structure (if applicable)
Survey Current property survey may be required for certain projects
Additional Info Any other documents requested by the Zoning Officer based on project type
4

Submit Application & Pay Fee

Submit your completed application with all required documents and payment to the Zoning Officer.

Residential Projects $50.00 permit fee
Commercial Projects $100.00 permit fee

Important: Do not begin any construction or work until your permit is approved and issued.

5

Application Review

The Zoning Officer will review your application to ensure compliance with all zoning regulations:

  • Zoning district compliance: Verifies the proposed use is permitted in your zoning district
  • Setback requirements: Ensures proper distances from property lines
  • Building height limits: Confirms structure meets height restrictions
  • Lot coverage: Checks that total building coverage doesn't exceed limits
  • Parking requirements: Verifies adequate parking is provided

Possible Outcomes:

Approved: Permit is issued and you may begin work

Conditional Approval: Permit approved with specific conditions to be met

Additional Information Needed: Zoning Officer requests more documentation

Denied: Project does not comply with zoning regulations (variance may be required)

6

Receive Permit & Begin Work

Once approved, you'll receive your zoning permit. You may now begin construction according to the approved plans.

  • Post the permit: Display permit at the job site in a visible location
  • Follow approved plans: Construction must match submitted and approved plans
  • Schedule inspections: Contact the Zoning Officer for required inspections
  • Report changes: Any modifications to approved plans must be submitted for review

Remember: The zoning permit must be obtained BEFORE starting any work. Beginning construction without a permit may result in stop-work orders and fines.

7

Inspections & Project Completion

During and after construction, the Zoning Officer may conduct inspections to ensure compliance:

  • Progress inspections: May be conducted during construction phases
  • Final inspection: Required upon project completion
  • Certificate of Occupancy: May be issued for completed structures
  • Documentation: Keep records of inspections and approvals

Coordinate with the Zoning Officer to schedule all required inspections. Do not occupy or use the structure until final approval is granted.

✓ Project Complete!

Your project is now complete and in compliance with Clinton Township zoning regulations.

Processing Timeline

Typical Review Time: Most straightforward applications are reviewed within 5-10 business days of submission.

Complex Projects: Larger or more complex projects may require additional review time.

Expedited Review: Contact the Zoning Officer to discuss urgent projects or time-sensitive situations.

Need Help?

Questions about the permit process? Contact the Zoning Officer before submitting your application. They can help determine what permits you need and guide you through the process.
